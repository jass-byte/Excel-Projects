# Walmart Sales Analysis & Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Walmart's sales data to identify business trends, evaluate sales performance, and uncover meaningful insights across different product categories, states, and years.

An interactive Sales Dashboard was developed to visualize key performance indicators (KPIs), track order trends, and analyze sales distribution across various business segments.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance and order trends.
- Identify top-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Evaluate sales distribution across different states.
- Compare yearly sales performance to identify growth patterns.
- Discover business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales:** 3,701,832
- **Total Orders:** 3,203

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

1. **Yearly Sales Performance:** 2014 recorded the highest sales of 1,314,869, followed by 2013 with 912,631.

2. **Order Trends:** 2014 recorded the highest number of orders (1,099), indicating stronger order activity compared to previous years.

3. **Geographical Analysis:** California emerged as the leading state, generating sales of 2,375,785, followed by Washington with 725,763.

4. **Product Category Analysis:** Tables, Phones, Chairs, and Accessories were among the leading product categories in terms of sales.

5. **Top-Performing Categories:** Tables generated 502,085 in sales, followed by Phones at 494,831 and Chairs at 450,011.

6. **Regional Performance:** Significant differences in sales across states highlight opportunities to improve regional sales strategies and market penetration.

## 🛠️ Dashboard Features
- Interactive year filters.
- State-wise sales analysis.
- Product category performance visualization.
- Yearly order trend analysis.
- Total sales and order KPI cards.
- Dynamic charts for comparing business performance.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing product categories to maximize revenue.
- Explore growth opportunities in states with relatively lower sales.
- Analyze yearly sales trends to improve forecasting and inventory planning.
- Develop targeted marketing strategies for underperforming regions.

## 🎯 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Exploratory Data Analysis and interactive data visualization can transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.
